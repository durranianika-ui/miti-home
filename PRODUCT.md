## Register

brand

## Users

Miti Home serves design-conscious customers across the UAE — people with a modern lifestyle who seek experiences and value quality over quantity. They notice details, care how a room looks and feels, host and entertain at home, and would rather own fewer, better things. Many discover us on mobile through social content and expect the site to feel as considered as the products. The same codebase supports internal admins managing the catalogue, categories, collections, inventory, coupons, customers, orders, campaigns and merchandising.

## Product Purpose

Miti Home is a Dubai-based home lifestyle brand that curates beautiful, useful and unexpected products for modern living — statement décor, unique lighting, smart storage and organisation, home entertainment, and "Why didn't I have this before?" finds. The storefront presents them through SEO-friendly public routes, editorial product presentation, curated collections, account-backed wishlists, and a checkout that prices in AED (VAT-inclusive), delivers within the UAE, supports cash on delivery and accepts cards through a hosted payment provider when configured.

Success means a customer understands the brand instantly, browses comfortably on mobile, trusts prices, delivery terms and stock, and completes checkout feeling they have bought from a luxury house — not a marketplace.

**Promise:** Beautiful spaces. A better you.
**Vision:** To become the leading home lifestyle brand in the UAE and beyond — known for discovering beautiful, useful and unexpected products that transform everyday living.
**Mission:** To help people create beautiful spaces that inspire a better life through thoughtfully curated products and exceptional experiences.

## Brand Personality

Modern luxury living — warm, calm and timeless. Not just a store, a lifestyle. The interface is ivory-grounded, image-led and quietly confident, with charcoal type and a signature gold used sparingly as a mark of care: hairline rules, the brand diamond, focus and hover accents.

Principles from the brand pack: curated, not crowded; design-led and lifestyle-focused; quality over quantity; unique and unexpected finds; strong content and community; a luxury experience at every touchpoint.

## Anti-references

Miti Home doesn't sell everything, doesn't compete on price, doesn't compromise on quality, doesn't follow trends blindly and doesn't look like a generic store. Avoid anything that reads as a discount marketplace, dropshipping template or cluttered e-commerce theme: constant promotional banners, countdown timers, flashing sale badges, crowded product cards, loud colour, fake scarcity, fake reviews or invented social proof, excessive glassmorphism, and motion that competes with the products.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the pieces: product photography, generous whitespace and clean typography carry the brand more than decorative chrome.
- Calm, disciplined motion: purposeful transitions, paused offscreen work, reduced-motion alternatives, never several heavy effects in one viewport.
- Protect trust at checkout: server-verified pricing, stock, coupon rules and payment status matter more than client convenience. Show only payment methods that can actually take money.
- Configuration over copy: commercial terms, contact details and legal identifiers come from `lib/constants.ts` and `lib/brand.ts`, so the owner can change them without code edits. Hide anything that is not configured rather than showing a placeholder.
- Optimise for real mobile shoppers: public pages stay cacheable, fast, SEO-safe and resilient in social in-app browsers.
- Preserve early-stage clarity: fix root causes directly and avoid backward-compatibility shims unless the product explicitly needs them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, meaningful alt text describing each piece, readable contrast (use `text-brand-strong` for gold text on ivory; decorative `--brand` gold is for fills and rules only), and reduced-motion alternatives across public and admin UI. Colour is supportive, never the only signal of state. Touch targets stay comfortable on mobile, layouts hold at 375px without horizontal scroll, and customer copy uses plain UK/UAE English.
