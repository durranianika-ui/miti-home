# Miti Home — Design Philosophy & Engineering Aesthetics

This document defines the design philosophy, visual system, motion rules and interactive patterns of the Miti Home storefront. Read it before creating, modifying or refactoring any client-side UI. Brand and audience context lives in `PRODUCT.md`; engineering rules live in `AGENTS.md`.

---

## 1. Brand Personality & Core Intent

*   **Modern luxury living — warm, calm and timeless.** Miti Home is a Dubai-based home lifestyle brand ("Luxury Living — Beautiful spaces. A better you."). The site should feel like walking into a quietly confident design house, not browsing a catalogue.
*   **Curated, not crowded.** Fewer elements, more space. Let one beautiful image or one well-set line of type carry a section. Every block should earn its place.
*   **Gold is a signature, not a paint.** The brand gold (`--brand`, `#C8A96A`) marks care and craft: hairline rules, the brand diamond, small eyebrows, hover and focus accents, the occasional fill behind charcoal text. It is never a background for large areas or long passages of text.
*   **Anti-reference:** never look like a discount marketplace or generic store. No countdown timers, flashing badges, "SALE" shouting, crowded cards, fake urgency or invented social proof. Value is communicated through curation, photography and service — not price.

## 2. Aesthetic System & Tokens

All tokens are defined in `app/globals.css` (Tailwind 4 `@theme`); brand hex values are mirrored in `BRAND.colors` in `lib/brand.ts` for emails and generated assets.

### Colour
*   **Light (default):** ivory ground `--background #F7F5F0`, charcoal ink `--foreground #141312`, warm surfaces `--secondary #EFEBE4` / `--muted #ECE7DF`, muted text `--muted-foreground #675D53`, hairlines `--border #E2DBD0`. Supporting neutrals: beige `#D6CEC1`, taupe `#8C7F72`.
*   **Gold:** `--brand #C8A96A` is decorative (fills, rules, the diamond); `--brand-strong` is the text-safe gold (`#8A6B32` on ivory, 4.9:1). Use `text-brand-strong` for gold text, `bg-brand` for fills paired with `text-neutral-950`.
*   **Dark mode:** available through the ThemeProvider; tokens are redefined under `.dark`. Build with tokens (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `border-border`) so both themes work. Sections that must stay charcoal in both themes use fixed neutrals (`bg-neutral-950 text-neutral-50`).
*   **Surfaces:** solid, calm fills. No colourful gradients, heavy glassmorphism or drop-shadow stacks. A faint grain overlay is acceptable.

### Typography
*   **Display — Montserrat Light** (`--font-montserrat`, weights 300–600). `.font-display` for mixed-case headlines (weight 300, slight positive tracking); `.font-display-caps` for wide-tracked uppercase headlines ("A MORE BEAUTIFUL EVERYDAY").
*   **Labels — Montserrat** via `.font-heading`: uppercase micro-labels, e.g. `font-heading text-[10px] font-medium uppercase tracking-[0.34em] text-brand-strong`. Buttons use `font-heading text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.22em]`.
*   **Body — Lato** (`--font-lato`, `font-sans`, the default): `text-sm leading-7` / `text-base leading-8` for reading copy, `text-muted-foreground` for secondary text.
*   **Wordmark:** `components/brand/wordmark.tsx` ("MITI HOME"); never re-typeset the logo by hand.
*   **Pricing:** always formatted with `formatPrice()` from `lib/money.ts` ("AED 1,250"), tabular numerals where prices align.

### Signature details
*   **Brand rule:** `.brand-rule` — a gold hairline with a centred diamond, from the logo lock-up:
    `<div className="brand-rule w-40 text-[10px]"><span className="h-1.5 w-1.5 rotate-45 bg-brand" /></div>`. Use it to open or close a section, not between every block.
*   **Diamond bullets:** `h-1 w-1 rotate-45 bg-brand` (or `h-1.5 w-1.5`) instead of round bullets.
*   **Primary CTA:** square corners, `h-12 px-7 bg-foreground text-background font-heading text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.22em] hover:bg-brand hover:text-neutral-950`. Secondary CTA: the same with `border border-foreground` on a transparent ground.

### Layout rhythm
*   **Airy sections:** `py-16 md:py-24` minimum (up to `md:py-28` for statements); gutters `px-4 sm:px-6 md:px-12`.
*   **Measured containers:** `max-w-3xl` for reading pages (policies), `max-w-6xl`/`max-w-7xl` for editorial grids.
*   **Product framing:** borderless cards, `3:4` or `4:5` images on a `bg-muted` placeholder, name and price set quietly underneath.
*   **Mobile first:** every page must hold at 375px with no horizontal scroll; hairline-bordered grids collapse to one column.

## 3. Interactive Mechanics & Page Studies

The motion system is retained from the storefront's original build and re-skinned for Miti Home. Keep the mechanics; keep them calm.

### A. Draggable Hero (`components/features/hero.tsx`)
*   A split editorial hero where the active slide dominates and neighbouring slides show as cropped previews.
*   The active slide supports horizontal drag (`drag="x"`, elastic constraints); clicking a side panel advances.
*   Titles and details enter with a short blur-to-sharp stagger (`blur(6px)` → `0`) on an expo-out ease; reduced motion renders them instantly. Playback pauses offscreen via `IntersectionObserver`.

### B. Draggable Infinite Canvas Gallery (`components/features/gallery-client.tsx`)
*   **Virtualised tiles:** a coordinate grid is rendered only around the viewport; dragging translates the canvas (`translate3d`) with eased follow and inertial momentum on release.
*   **Scatter-to-gather intro:** tiles in view start at the centre, scaled up with random rotation, then settle into the grid. Dragging is locked until the intro finishes.
*   **FLIP lightbox:** clicking a tile morphs it from its on-screen rect to a centred (desktop) or full-screen (mobile) card; the product name reveals word by word (`yPercent: 110 → 0`), then the category · price line and a square "View the piece" CTA. Escape or a click outside reverses the animation.
*   Tiles carry `data-miti-gallery-card`. Reduced motion skips the intro and the FLIP, showing the lightbox statically.

### C. Angled Moving Band (`components/features/angled-gallery-band.tsx`)
*   Rows of imagery rotated (`14deg` mobile, `28deg` desktop) and scaled to span the section; alternating rows drift in opposite directions with scroll via GSAP ScrollTrigger. Paused offscreen; static under reduced motion.

### D. Scroll-Directional Marquee (`components/effects/directional-marquee.tsx`)
*   A text marquee whose direction follows scroll: scrolling down plays forward, scrolling up reverses (`timeScale -1`) and flips its glyphs. Paused offscreen; static under reduced motion.

### E. Mobile Navigation Reveal (`app/navbar.tsx`)
*   A full-screen editorial menu revealed with a GSAP polygon clip-path sweep while the page beneath tilts and shifts for depth. Focusing or hovering an item swaps a preview image. Reduced motion falls back to a fade.

### F. Search Overlay (`app/navbar.tsx`)
*   A full-screen, typography-first overlay ("Search Miti Home") with debounced suggestions, inline product matches with imagery and suggested terms.

### G. Shop the Space (`components/features/shop-the-space.tsx`)
*   A horizontal snap-scroller of room scenes (still image or short vertical video). Tapping a video unmutes it and mutes all others; a product card overlay links to the pieces in the scene. Videos play only while in view.

### H. Scroll Reveals (`components/ui/scroll-reveal.tsx`)
*   `ScrollReveal`, `StaggerContainer`, `StaggerItem` fade content up with a light blur on first view. Use them for below-the-fold sections only — never on the LCP image or above-the-fold headline.

## 4. Performance & Engineering Guidelines

Luxury visuals must never cost page performance.

1.  **Intersection Observer lifecycle:** every animation loop (bands, marquees, carousels) and every video must pause as soon as it leaves the viewport. Kill or disable ScrollTriggers while the navigation drawer is open.
2.  **Reduced motion:** always respect `prefers-reduced-motion` via `useReducedMotion()` (Framer Motion). Bypass heavy translations, 3D transforms, inertial drags and clip-path sweeps; use instant state changes or simple fades.
3.  **Scroll & state restoration:** catalogue pages (`ShopClient`) store scroll offset, filters and visible count in `sessionStorage` before navigating to a product and restore them on return.
4.  **Debounce & cancel:** search inputs debounce (≥ 250ms) and cancel stale requests with `AbortController`.
5.  **Dynamic imports:** heavy interactive sections (reels, canvases, sliders) load via `next/dynamic` with a quiet skeleton.
6.  **Images:** use `next/image` with accurate `sizes`, `priority` only for the LCP image, and descriptive alt text that names the piece and its material or setting.
